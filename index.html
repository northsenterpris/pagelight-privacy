Pagelight Privacy Policy

Pagelight: Reading Tracker for iPhone · Effective 12 November 2026

The short version

  • There's no account. Your books and reading history stay on your iPhone.
  • Searching or scanning a book sends only the search text or ISBN to Open Library.
  • Anonymous usage statistics are off unless you turn them on. They never include what you read.
  • No ads, no tracking, and nothing is sold.

Who we are

Pagelight is published by Mia Ipsen ("we"), who is responsible for the data described here. Contact: ipsenmia90@gmail.com.

Data that stays on your iPhone

Your library (titles, authors, page counts, formats, covers), reading logs, streaks, yearly goal, reminder time and settings are stored only in the app's database on your device. We have no server and cannot see this data. Pagelight does not use iCloud. Your data may be included in your own iPhone backup, which Apple handles under its own privacy policy.

Book search and covers (Open Library)

To find books and covers, Pagelight contacts Open Library, a service of the Internet Archive. Pagelight sends:

  • the text you type into search, or the ISBN from a barcode you scan or enter, and
  • requests for cover images of books you add.

Nothing else from your library or reading history is sent. As with any internet request, Open Library receives your device's IP address. Requests identify the app (name, version and a contact address) so Open Library can reach us. See the Internet Archive's terms and privacy policy. If you never search or scan, Pagelight never contacts Open Library.

Anonymous usage statistics (optional)

If you choose "Share Anonymous Usage Data" (during setup or later in Settings), Pagelight sends usage statistics to Google Analytics for Firebase, run by Google as our service provider. This is off by default. Until you opt in, Firebase is not started and nothing is sent or saved for later.

When it's on, we receive:

  • which features are used, for example "a book was added by scanning", "progress was logged" or "a screen was opened";
  • coarse categories only, such as a book's format (print, e-book or audio) or a streak range like "7–29 days";
  • standard technical information Firebase collects, such as app version, iOS version, device model, a random app-instance ID, and approximate location (country or region) derived from your IP address.

We never send titles, authors, ISBNs, search text, page numbers, percentages, exact counts, reading dates or anything you type. Pagelight does not collect the advertising identifier (IDFA), does not use the data for advertising, and does not track you across other apps or websites.

Google keeps this data for 2 months, then deletes it. Google may process it outside your country, including in the United States, under the safeguards in Google's data processing terms. See also Google's privacy policy.

Turning it off: go to Settings → Privacy and switch off "Share Anonymous Usage Data". Collection stops straight away, and the analytics data stored on your device, including the app-instance ID, is reset.

Reminders, camera and notifications

  • Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your iPhone. Nothing goes through a server. You can turn them off in Pagelight or in iOS Settings.
  • The camera is used only to read a book's barcode, on the device. No photos or video are saved or sent.

Export and deleting your data

  • Export: Settings → Your Data creates a CSV or JSON file of your library and logs. It goes only where you choose to share or save it.
  • Delete All Data: Settings → Your Data erases your library, logs and covers from the app. Your usage-data choice, the fact that setup was completed, and a record of the app's original install version are kept, so deleting never changes your privacy setting.
  • Deleting the app removes everything Pagelight stored on your iPhone.

Legal basis (EU/EEA and UK)

Usage statistics are processed only with your consent, which you can withdraw at any time in Settings. Book lookups are processed because you ask for them, to provide the feature you're using. You have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the processing of your personal data, to object, and to complain to your local data protection authority. Because usage data is not linked to your name or any account, we usually can't identify which records belong to you. Turning off sharing is the most effective way to stop processing. For any request, email us.

Children

Pagelight is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal data from them. Usage statistics stay off unless someone turns them on.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the effective date above. If we ever want to collect new kinds of data, we'll ask for your consent first where it's required.
