Common questions

Where is my data stored?
Only on your iPhone. There's no account and no cloud sync. Use Settings → Your Data → Export to keep a copy.

Search or scanning doesn't find my book.
Book data comes from Open Library and needs an internet connection. If a book isn't there, choose Add Manually. Everything else works offline.

How do I turn usage statistics on or off?
Settings → Privacy → Share Anonymous Usage Data. See the privacy policy for what is and isn't shared.

How do I delete everything?
Settings → Your Data → Delete All Data, or delete the app.
